About Rottenberg's

Heimish • Elegant • Unforgettable — kosher catering from the heart of Williamsburg, Brooklyn.

Who we are

Heimish cooking,
elegant presentation

Rottenberg's Catering is a heimish kosher caterer based at 264 Hewes St in Williamsburg, Brooklyn, serving the New York community. We cook the way a simcha deserves — real heimish taste, prepared fresh — and we present it the way a simcha should look: beautifully plated and elegantly set.

Whether it's a Shabbos meal, a sheva brochos, a kiddush, or a weekday event, we handle the food and the table so you can be a guest at your own simcha.

  • Complete Shabbos menus — night & day meals
  • Sheva brochos, simchas, kiddushim & weekday events
  • Linens & napkins in dozens of colors, including our textured Bark Collection
  • Heimish challos available upon request
